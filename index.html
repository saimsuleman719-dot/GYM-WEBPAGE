FUEL YOUR PROGRESS

YOUR GOAL. YOUR FUEL.

Explore nutrition starting points for three common fitness goals.

01 / WEIGHT GAIN

LEAN GAIN PLAN

For people who want to gain weight gradually and support muscle development.

  • Eat regular, balanced meals and snacks.
  • Include protein, carbohydrates, and healthy fats.
  • Pair meals with progressive strength training.

Example: Oats with milk, eggs, rice, lentils, chicken or beans, yogurt, nuts, and fruit.

02 / FAT LOSS

LEANER LIFESTYLE

For people who want to reduce body fat while preserving strength and energy.

  • Use a moderate, sustainable calorie deficit.
  • Prioritize protein and fiber-rich foods.
  • Combine resistance training with daily movement.

Example: Eggs, fruit, chicken or beans, vegetables, whole grains, yogurt, and water.

03 / MUSCLE BUILDING

BULK & BUILD

For people looking to increase muscle size, strength, and workout performance.

  • Eat enough total energy to support training.
  • Spread protein across meals.
  • Plan recovery and gradually increase training loads.

Example: Oats, milk or soy milk, eggs or tofu, rice, fish or beans, potatoes, and fruit.

These are general examples, not personalized medical diets. Individual needs vary by age, body size, activity, health, and preferences. Consult a registered dietitian or qualified clinician for specific dietary or medical needs.
